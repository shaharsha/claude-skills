## What it does

Deletes `_REAL_PRODUCTS` — a hard-coded list of seven `(product_code, label, companies.name)` tuples
that was the product picker's real-client source, filtered by the serving database's `companies`
rows. The picker now reads `clients` through `api/services/client_registry.py` plus a declared demo
arm. `clients` gains `is_real` and `display_order`. CLI verbs `tenant add|list|update`.

## Invariants

- Simulated data must NEVER be presented as a real client's. The reverse is bad but less bad.
- A picker filter is presentation, never authorization.
- `/api/companies/products` drives the sidebar on every page load; one bad row must not 500 it.
- The pool is **autocommit** — no ambient transaction.
- Upgraded databases have NO `CHECK (is_real IN (0,1))`, and `idx_clients_product_code` is UNIQUE on
  the RAW code, so `ACME`/`acme`/`' ACME '` are distinct rows. Reason about the UPGRADED shape.
