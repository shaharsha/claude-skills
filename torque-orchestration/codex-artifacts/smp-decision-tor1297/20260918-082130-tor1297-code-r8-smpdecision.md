### 1. Simulator turns a hidden band edge into a visible series

`authored_pages/smp_simulator/charts.js:275-281` · **medium** · spec-violation · confidence 0.99

**Evidence.** The authored specification recreates the upper edge as `{ mark: 'line' ... }` and the lower edge as `{ mark: 'area' ... fill_to: 'previous' }`. The source builder declares both corresponding traces with `line=dict(width=0)` and hides the upper trace from the legend (`api/services/smp/loan_simulator.py:327-331`). `ChartView.tsx:1972-2005,2098-2115` draws the fill without a stroke but then draws the area trace’s Line, while the partner is also rendered as a normal Line. The README declares only the changed fill colour.

**Failure scenario.** Whenever cohort dispersion contains at least three observations, the ordinary initial payload or a recompute includes `sim-dispersion`. The published authored page shows boundary curves around the ±1σ region, including a full-strength `+1 std` curve and its extra legend entry; the builder-served Plotly chart shows a fill with those boundary traces at zero width and suppresses the upper legend entry.

**Suggested fix.** Represent and preserve `line.width: 0` and `showlegend: false` through the chart contract/compiler/renderer, then set them on these two authored series. If that fidelity cannot be implemented, declare both visible differences in the README.

### 2. Forecast draws outlines for ten width-zero band traces

`authored_pages/smp_forecast/charts.js:203-262` · **medium** · spec-violation · confidence 0.99

**Evidence.** The book band is emitted as a normal partner line plus an area at lines 205-207, and the same pair is repeated four times at lines 247-262. The builder sets all ten corresponding traces to `line=dict(width=0)` (`api/services/smp/forecast.py:1001-1006,1202-1205`). Readers see five uncertainty regions framed by added boundary curves, whereas the builder-served page intentionally renders all ten edges at zero width. This makes the regions look more crisply bounded than the source presentation.

**Suggested fix.** Add a supported no-stroke or zero-width series property and carry it for these ten traces. Also update the README’s difference list if any unavoidable visual divergence remains.
